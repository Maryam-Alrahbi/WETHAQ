# WETHAQ Digital Trust

WEBSITE DEVELOPMENT PROMPT: WETHAQ (وِثاق)

1. Project Overview

Create a professional, modern, responsive website for a startup called WETHAQ (وِثاق).

Project name: WETHAQ – Digital Trust & Verification Platform

Main tagline: Trust Every Digital Document.

Arabic tagline: وِثاق – لأن الثقة أساس كل معاملة رقمية.

Core concept:

WETHAQ is an AI-powered digital trust and fraud prevention platform designed to help individuals and businesses verify the authenticity and integrity of digital documents, initially focusing on invoices and payment requests.

The platform combines Artificial Intelligence (AI) with digital verification technologies to identify suspicious document modifications, detect potential impersonation, verify document sources, and explain possible risks in simple language.

The main goal is to make digital transactions safer and help users make more confident decisions before trusting or acting on a digital document.

The website should look like a real technology startup platform, not a university assignment or a basic demonstration website.

5. How It Works Page

Create a dedicated page explaining the platform's process in detail.

Use a visual step-by-step layout.

Step 1: Business Registration

A business creates an account and submits its basic business information.

The business profile should include:

Business name.

Business registration information.

Official email.

Official phone number.

Registered website or domain, if available.

Approved payment details.

The platform should include an appropriate verification process before marking a business as verified.

Step 2: Document Issuing

The registered business creates or uploads an invoice through the WETHAQ platform.

The invoice includes:

Invoice number.

Issue date.

Business information.

Customer information.

Description of products or services.

Total amount.

Payment information.

The system connects the issued document to a digital verification mechanism, such as a digital signature and QR code.

Step 3: Document Verification

The recipient can:

Scan the QR code.

Upload the invoice.

Enter a verification reference, if provided.

The platform checks whether the document matches the original verified version.

Step 4: AI Analysis

The AI system analyzes the document for suspicious indicators, including:

Changes in bank account details.

Inconsistent business information.

Unexpected modifications.

Suspicious links or contact information.

Unusual differences between document information and the verified business profile.

Step 5: Verification Result

Display the result in a clear and understandable format.

Possible results:

VERIFIED

The document matches the verified version and its source information.

WARNING

The system detected unusual information that requires additional review.

NOT VERIFIED

The document could not be matched with a valid verification record.

Important: Do not claim that a document is fraudulent simply because it cannot be verified. Explain that the result is inconclusive and requires independent confirmation.

12. Artificial Intelligence Integration

AI must be a meaningful part of the platform, not just a decorative feature.

The AI system should support:

Document content analysis.

Detection of suspicious inconsistencies.

Comparison of document information with registered business details.

Identification of unusual payment information.

Risk indicator classification.

Simple explanations of detected issues.

The AI should assist the verification process, not replace cryptographic verification or official source confirmation.

Use a combination of:

AI-powered document analysis.

OCR (Optical Character Recognition) to extract information from uploaded documents.

Digital signatures to support document integrity and source verification.

Secure verification records.

QR codes to make verification accessible.

Important technical distinction:

A digital signature can help confirm that a document has not changed since it was signed and that it is associated with a particular signing key. It does not automatically prove that the business itself is legitimate. Business identity verification and secure signing-key management are also required.

AI results should be treated as risk indicators rather than final legal or financial decisions.
